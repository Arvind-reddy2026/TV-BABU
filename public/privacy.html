DATA PROTECTION · NOTICE v1.0

Privacy &
your data rights.

This notice explains what personal information TV Babu Public Service collects through this website, why it is used, and how you can request access, correction, deletion or raise a privacy concern.

Last updated: 26 September 2026

1. Who handles your information

The TV Babu Public Service office operating this website is responsible for the personal information submitted through citizen services. For privacy questions, contact the office using the details published on the Contact section or submit the request form below.

2. Information we may collect

  • Account details such as your name and email address.
  • Contact details such as phone number, email, area or locality.
  • Information you submit in an issue, grievance, appointment request, event registration or contact message.
  • Booking references, event/appointment details and request status.
  • Basic technical information needed for authentication, session management, security and website operation.

Please avoid including sensitive personal information that is not necessary for your request.

3. Why we use it

We use submitted information to create and secure your account, receive and respond to public-service requests, manage event registrations and appointment approvals, provide reference numbers and tickets, communicate service updates, prevent misuse, and maintain website security.

We do not ask for consent to unrelated marketing in these service forms. If a separate purpose is introduced, it should be explained separately before collection.

4. Service providers, storage and security

The website uses Supabase for authentication, database and/or file storage services. Information may be processed by service providers supporting the website. Access controls are intended to limit personal request details to authorized administrators. No internet service can be guaranteed completely secure; suspected privacy or security incidents should be reported promptly.

5. Retention

Information should be retained only as long as needed to handle the request, meet applicable legal or operational requirements, resolve disputes and maintain necessary security records. The office should define and apply a documented retention and deletion schedule; this website notice does not itself automatically delete old records.

6. Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law and identity verification, you may request access to personal information, correction or updating, erasure where retention is no longer required, withdrawal of consent where processing relies on consent, or raise a privacy grievance. You may also contact the Data Protection Board of India through its official process where applicable.

We may ask for reasonable verification before acting on a request. Do not submit passwords or one-time codes in this form.

7. Submit a privacy request

Use this form to request access, correction, erasure, withdrawal of consent or to raise a privacy grievance. Include enough detail for the office to locate the relevant request, but do not include passwords or unnecessary sensitive data.

Submitting this form sends the details to the office for review. Please read this notice before submitting.

8. Legal and implementation note

The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the DPDP Rules, 2025 have phased commencement dates. This is a website privacy notice and request channel, not a certification of legal compliance. The office must separately confirm its legal identity and contact details, lawful processing grounds, retention periods, vendor arrangements, security and breach-response procedures, handling timelines, and any rules applicable to children or other special cases with qualified counsel.
