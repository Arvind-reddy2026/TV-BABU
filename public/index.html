Frequently asked questions

How do I book an appointment?

Choose “Book an appointment.” If you are not signed in, sign in or create a citizen account first, then submit your preferred date and request details.

How do I register for an event?

Open the Events section, choose an event with registration open, and select Register. Sign in if prompted, then complete the registration form.

How can I submit a public concern?

Use the grievance submission option in the public services area or contact the office using the details shown here.

Where can I find the office?

Click the office location in the Contact details to open its address in Google Maps.
